# Code of Conduct

StormAlert GH is an open-source civic technology project. We want discussion and contributions to be useful, respectful, and safe for people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but accept feedback when your words or actions cause harm.
- Keep criticism focused on the work, not the person.
- Be patient with newcomers and people learning the stack.
- Avoid harassment, threats, hate speech, and personal attacks.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions, issues, or pull requests.
- Security disclosures posted publicly before maintainers have had time to respond.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues; reject pull requests; or block participants who violate this code of conduct.

If you need to report a concern, contact the maintainers privately.
